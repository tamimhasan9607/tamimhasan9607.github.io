Featured VR Project

Beyond the Vape

An Anti-Vaping VR Experience

An immersive educational VR experience developed to raise awareness about vaping using Meta Quest 3, Unity, and Blender. Created as part of Federation University's Student Wellbeing initiative to reduce vaping among students aged 17-30 through engaging, game-based learning.

Features

Interactive Experience Elements

3D Anatomy Visualization

Interactive 3D scenes displaying accurate anatomical models of lungs, heart, and brain to demonstrate the physical impacts of vaping on the human body.

  • Real-time organ degradation
  • Before/after comparisons
  • Educational annotations

Interactive Vending Machines

Users interact with virtual vape vending machines and immediately witness the consequences, creating a cause-and-effect learning loop.

  • Hand-tracking interactions
  • Consequence simulations
  • Choice-based narratives

Immersive Audio Design

Professional voice narration and background music enhance user engagement and emotional connection to the educational content.

  • Spatial audio positioning
  • Guided voiceover
  • Ambient soundscapes

First-Time User Tutorial

Dedicated tutorial scene designed specifically for VR beginners, ensuring accessibility for all users regardless of technical experience.

  • Controller guidance
  • Comfort settings
  • Interactive onboarding

Agile Sprint Breakdown

Sprint 1

Research & Planning

Requirements gathering, user persona development, wireframing, and technical architecture design for the VR environment.

Sprint 2

Development & Integration

Core VR mechanics implementation, 3D asset creation in Blender, Unity scene building, and initial user testing iterations.

Sprint 3

Polish & Deployment

Audio integration, UI/UX refinement, performance optimization for Meta Quest 3, final QA testing, and presentation preparation.
